About AMK Care Service

Care shaped around dignity, comfort and family peace of mind

AMK Care Service is a compassionate home care provider delivering live in care across England and home care in selected local areas.

We help people remain safe, comfortable and independent at home, while respecting their choices, routines and dignity.

Who we are

A personal care service built around people

Every person has their own way of living, their own routines and their own family priorities. AMK Care Service begins by listening, then builds support around what matters most.

Our carers are carefully selected, professionally trained and committed to delivering respectful, compassionate care every day.

Our aim

To help people remain independent, safe and comfortable in the place they know best.

Our promise

To communicate clearly, treat every person with respect and provide support that feels calm, reliable and personal.

Our values

What guides the care we provide

Dignity

Care is delivered with privacy, respect and sensitivity.

Choice

Support is built around routines, preferences and personal wishes.

Independence

We encourage people to remain involved in daily life where possible.

Peace of mind

Families receive clear communication and practical next steps.

Our approach

Simple, careful and family focused

  1. ListenWe learn about the person, the home, the family and the support needed.
  2. PlanWe shape care around routines, preferences, risks and comfort.
  3. SupportCare is delivered with kindness, respect and clear communication.
  4. ReviewSupport can be discussed and adjusted when needs change.
