# Mobile final QA

This note records the final mobile-only polish applied after checking the live homepage in an iPhone in-app browser.

Changes are intentionally small and do not alter the approved structure, wording, CRM integration or desktop design.

- service cards return to natural height on narrow screens, removing unnecessary empty space
- service icons and mobile card spacing are more balanced
- service links remain easy to tap without becoming oversized
- the fixed Call / WhatsApp bar is slimmer, respects iPhone safe areas and leaves more usable screen space
- the mobile menu is height-limited and scrollable on short screens
- the family-time section is tightened slightly for clearer reading on narrow screens

The current website should remain the stable public version after this mobile correction.
