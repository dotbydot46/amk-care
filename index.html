Our approach

Care that fits the person, not the other way around

We take time to understand the person, their home, personality, routines, favourite meals, mobility, health needs and the support that matters most.

The aim is to make care feel calm and natural, while helping each person remain as comfortable, confident and independent as possible.

  • Dignity, choice and familiar routines
  • Thoughtful attention to nutrition and hydration
  • Safe mobility support in line with the care plan
  • Regular communication with family members

An example of tailored care

Listening closely and adjusting everyday support

One family told us their loved one was experiencing stressful mealtimes alongside recurring nosebleeds and concerns around choking. AMK Care worked closely with the family, followed the agreed care plan and relevant professional guidance, reduced avoidable stress around meals and introduced more varied food choices suited to the person’s needs and preferences.

The family reported that the situation became calmer and easier to manage. This anonymised example is based on family feedback; every person’s needs and outcomes are different.

Care is provided in line with the agreed care plan and does not replace medical advice or clinical assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for families

Clear information about arranging personalised care at home.

What is live in care?

A carer lives in the person’s home and provides support according to an agreed care plan, helping them remain in familiar surroundings.

Do you provide care throughout the UK?

AMK Care Service currently advertises live in care across England and home care in selected local areas. Contact the team if support is needed elsewhere in the UK so current availability can be checked.

How is care tailored to the individual?

The assessment considers needs, routines, preferences, nutrition, mobility, risks, communication and family expectations. These details are used to shape the care plan.

Can carers help with meals and nutrition?

Where included in the care plan, carers may support meal planning, shopping, preparation, hydration and encouragement, taking account of dietary needs and professional guidance.

Can carers help with mobility?

Where assessed as safe and appropriate, carers may assist with walking, transfers and agreed movement routines in line with the care plan and any relevant professional guidance.

How much does care cost?

Costs depend on the type and level of support required. A personalised quotation is provided after a free assessment.

How quickly can care begin?

This depends on the person’s needs, location, assessment and suitable carer availability. Contact the team to discuss current availability.

Are carers DBS checked?

AMK Care Service uses Enhanced DBS checks as part of its safer recruitment approach.

Does a live in carer need a private room?

Yes. A suitable private bedroom and reasonable access to bathroom and kitchen facilities are required.

How do I get started?

Complete the consultation form, call, email or contact AMK Care Service through WhatsApp. The team will explain the next step.
